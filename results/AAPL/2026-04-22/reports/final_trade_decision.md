**Why Not Hold**
`Hold` is not justified here. A true hold requires either:
- Clear favorable asymmetry despite uncertainty, or
- No better action because the evidence is balanced

Neither condition applies. The downside catalysts are concrete now:
- Premium valuation
- AI monetization still not concrete
- CEO transition uncertainty
- China and regulatory overhang
- A technically fragile setup around `266`

By contrast, the upside catalysts are mostly narrative-dependent and future-dated. The aggressive case is fundamentally a bet that the market will reward possibilities before proof. That can happen, but it is not the higher-probability path for risk-controlled capital. As the conservative analyst put it, "`waiting for proof is not missing the trade in a conservative framework; it is filtering out low-visibility bets.`"

Past mistake to avoid: do not confuse business durability with stock attractiveness. That lesson is directly applicable here. Apple’s quality is not in dispute. The mistake would be giving the stock credit today for AI/product acceleration and leadership renewal that have not yet shown up in monetizable execution.
